name: Manual UPI checkout
description: The project owner's checkout, inventory, and payment verification requirements.
---

**Rule:** Keep the full local catalog orderable without Shopify. Checkout uses the supplied UPI QR and sends order details through WhatsApp; UPI payment remains unverified until the studio confirms it.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested website purchases with UPI QR payment and WhatsApp order details instead of Shopify checkout, and wants the listed products to be buyable.

**How to apply:** Preserve the local catalog and manual confirmation flow. Do not claim a payment is complete from a buyer-entered transaction reference; verify it with the studio before fulfillment.
